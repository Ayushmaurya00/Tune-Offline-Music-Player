Answers & Details

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Tune require an internet connection?

No! Tune is 100% offline first. All audio playback, equalizer processing, waveform rendering, and playlist management happen locally on your computer or phone. Internet access is only optionally used when fetching online album cover art or synchronized lyrics.

What audio formats are supported?

Tune supports all major lossless and lossy audio codecs including MP3, FLAC, M4A, WAV, OGG, AAC, OPUS, and MP4 audio.

How does the Spotify Playlist Importer work?

Paste any public Spotify playlist URL into Tune. Tune extracts the tracklist and automatically scans your offline music library to match songs. You can then download missing songs through your preferred tools.

Is Tune free and open source?

Yes, Tune is completely free under the open-source MIT license. There are no paywalls, locked features, tracking analytics, or hidden charges.
