Frequently asked questions.

Does Tune send telemetry or audio metrics?

No. The application contains zero telemetry daemons, tracking pixels, or user behavior analytics. All database indexes, listening history, and playlist tables reside strictly on your local disk.

Which audio codecs are decoded natively?

Tune decodes FLAC (up to 24-bit 192kHz), ALAC, WAV, MP3 (CBR/VBR up to 320kbps), M4A, AAC, OGG, and OPUS audio formats without forced downsampling.

How does the Spotify playlist importer operate offline?

It scrapes public track titles and artist names from the shared URL and executes a client-side fuzzy match against your local audio files. Missing files are flagged so you can populate your offline folder.
